Asteroids Deluxe — vector shapes

Every display-list block in $4D80–$57FF, executed the way the DVG would: JSRL is followed into sub-shapes, and intensity sets opacity. Names come from the Atari vector source. Generated by mkpreview.py.

lit vector    blanked (z=0) — the beam moving dark between marks. A shape brackets its picture with blanked moves in and out of position; those are not drawn, because they start from wherever the caller left the beam rather than from anywhere in the shape. Shapes tagged blanked are stored with z=0 throughout and the 6502 ORs the intensity in as it copies them (CPYVEC) — they are drawn lit here, because that is how they reach the screen.
